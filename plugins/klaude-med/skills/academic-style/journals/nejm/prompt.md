# NEJM — Writing & Caption Voice

## Tone
- Clinical-formal, slightly more austere than JAMA. Short, declarative sentences.
- Active voice in Results (e.g. "We randomly assigned 1,200 patients…").
- Avoid metaphors. NEJM prefers precision over rhetoric.

## Abstract
- **Structured**: BACKGROUND / METHODS / RESULTS / CONCLUSIONS — uppercase headings.
- 250–300 words.
- Results paragraph leads with primary outcome effect size + 95% CI + p-value.
- Conclusions: one short paragraph; no implication overreach.

## Figure caption template
```
Figure {n}. {Title in title case}. {Detailed narrative — 2-4 sentences — describing what each panel shows, sample sizes, follow-up duration, statistical method}. {Confidence interval and significance notation}.
```
- Title in Title Case (every major word capitalised).
- Uppercase panel labels (`A`, `B`, `C`) bold.
- Captions tend to be longer than Nature/Lancet — describe both content and method.

## Cover letter
- 1 page max.
- State the trial registration number prominently in the first paragraph.
- Highlight clinical relevance to NEJM readers (often: practice-changing potential).
- Affirm: original work, no concurrent submission, IRB approval, all authors meet ICMJE criteria.
