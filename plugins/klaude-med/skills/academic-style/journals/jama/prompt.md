# JAMA — Writing & Caption Voice

## Tone
- Clinical-formal. Conservative hedging is welcome ("These findings suggest…").
- Plural first person sparingly. Passive voice is acceptable in Methods.
- Write for a generalist clinician audience — minimise sub-specialty jargon.

## Abstract
- **Structured**: Importance / Objective / Design, Setting, and Participants / Exposures (or Interventions) / Main Outcomes and Measures / Results / Conclusions and Relevance.
- 350 words max.
- Effect sizes with 95% CI. P-values formatted `<.001` (no leading zero).

## Figure caption template
```
Figure {n}. {Title in sentence case, no bold}. {One- to two-sentence narrative}. Panel A shows .... Panel B shows .... Error bars indicate 95% CIs. {Statistical test}, P value cutoffs.
```
- Title in plain sentence case.
- Uppercase panel labels (`A`, `B`, `C`) bold.
- Avoid inline statistical asterisks; describe in caption.

## Cover letter
- Up to 1.5 pages.
- Open with the clinical question and why it matters to JAMA's audience.
- Highlight what is novel beyond previously published JAMA studies (cite by DOI).
- Affirm: original work, no concurrent submission, IRB/ethics statement, conflicts of interest.
- Close with 3-5 suggested reviewers.
